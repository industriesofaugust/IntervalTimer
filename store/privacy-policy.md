# Privacy Policy — Dialed

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Dialed ("the app"), an interval timer for workouts, is published by August. It has a phone app and a
Wear OS watch app. This policy explains what they do with your information.
The short version: your workouts stay on your own devices, and nothing is sent
to the publisher or to anyone else.

## What the app collects

Nothing. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no crash reporting.
There is no server: the publisher never receives any of your data. Neither
the phone app nor the watch app asks for the internet permission, so they
cannot send data anywhere themselves.

## What the app stores on your devices

So that it is there the next time you open the app, it saves on your phone
and watch:

- the workouts (templates) you build;
- your workout history: when each workout happened, its intervals, how long it
  ran, and, when the watch measured them, heart rate, steps and calories;
- your settings, including your age if you choose to enter it (used only to
  work out heart-rate zones).

## Heart rate, steps and calories

During a workout the watch app reads your heart rate, steps and calories from
the watch's sensors, with your permission. They are read only while a workout
is running, shown on screen, and saved with that workout. You can refuse the
permission; the timer then works without them.

## Phone and watch

The phone and watch apps keep in step through Google's Wear OS data layer,
part of Google Play services on your devices. Your templates, workout results
and settings travel between your own phone and your own watch this way. When
the two are not directly connected, Google may pass this data between them
through your Google account. It goes only to your devices.

## Health Connect

If you choose to link Health Connect, the app:

- writes each finished workout to Health Connect as an exercise session, with
  its work, rest and pause segments, and the heart rate, steps and calories
  the watch measured;
- reads your most recent resting heart rate, only to draw heart-rate zones on
  your workout charts.

Nothing else is read. Health Connect data stays on your phone, under your
control: you decide which other apps may see it. Deleting a workout in the app
also deletes its Health Connect records. You can stop sharing at any time in
the app's Settings or in Health Connect.

Health data is used only to show you your own workouts. It is never used for
advertising, never sold, and never shared with anyone.

## Spoken cues

If you switch on voice cues, the names of your exercises are handed to your
phone's text-to-speech engine to be read aloud. That engine is part of your
device and works under its own privacy policy.

## Backups made by Android

If you have turned on Android's own backup in your device settings, Android
may include this app's data in the backup it keeps in your Google account, and
restore it when you set up a new device. That backup is made and controlled by
Android and Google, not by the app or its publisher, who never receive it. You
can turn it off in your device's backup settings.

## Permissions

Phone:

- Notifications: to show a running workout with pause, skip and end buttons,
  and to tell you when a workout on your watch can be followed on the phone.
- Run in the background during a workout: so the countdown and its cues keep
  going when the screen is off or another app is in front. It stops when the
  workout ends.
- Vibration: for the vibration cues.
- Health Connect: only if you link it, as described above.

Watch:

- Body sensors (heart rate) and physical activity: to record heart rate, steps
  and calories during a workout.
- Notifications and running in the background during a workout: so the timer
  keeps counting and cueing with the screen off.
- Vibration: for the vibration cues.

## Children

The app is a workout timer with no data collection. It is not directed at
children.

## Deleting your data

You can delete any workout from its page or from the history list. Uninstalling
the app, or clearing its storage in Android's settings, deletes everything it
has stored on that device. Records already written to Health Connect can be
removed in Health Connect.
