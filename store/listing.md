## Full description (4000 max)

Interval Timer is a workout timer built around one idea: finish the interval you are in. A segmented ring shows the whole session at a glance, the countdown is big enough to read from across the room, and the app tells you what is next before it arrives.

BUILD A WORKOUT IN SECONDS
• Quick session: set rounds, work and rest, and go
• Starter workouts for Tabata, HIIT and strength
• A template builder for anything else: name each exercise, set each time, drag to reorder, repeat the whole list
• Every workout shows its shape as a coloured strip before you start

A TIMER YOU CAN LEAVE ALONE
• Keeps counting with the screen off or with your music app in front
• A notification with the countdown and pause, skip and end buttons
• Beeps, vibration and an optional voice that says each exercise as it starts
• Your music is turned down for a moment during each cue, then comes back
• A 3-2-1 countdown into every change

ON YOUR WRIST
• A full Wear OS app: start any workout from the watch, or let the watch join a workout you start on the phone
• Heart rate, steps and calories recorded by the watch
• Pause, skip and end from either device; the two stay in step

LOOK BACK
• History of every workout, with time, intervals completed and a heart-rate chart
• Heart-rate zones and time in each zone
• This week and this month at a glance
• Optional Health Connect link, so workouts appear in your other health apps

YOURS
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• No server: your workouts stay on your phone and watch
• Light and dark themes, and two timer styles
